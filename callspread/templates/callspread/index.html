    Alerting hook is ready. Each rule below is evaluated on every poll server-side data refresh. Wire a transport (Slack / Telegram / email / webhook) into src/alerts.js and rules will fire on state transitions — tell me the channel and thresholds when you're ready.
    Greeks come straight from STS per position — no re-pricing in this dashboard. Raw per-contract greeks are recovered by inverting STS's money-scaling: Delta = Delta $ / (qty × spot), Gamma = Gamma $ / (qty × 0.01 × spot²), Vega = Vega $ / qty, Theta = Theta $ / qty.
    Gamma $ is the change in Delta $ per 1% spot move. Vega $ is per 1 vol point. Theta $ is per day. Rho $ is per 1% rate.
    Blended delta = net delta (CC units) ÷ total CC held. Coverage reserves the full short-leg notional 1:1 — deliberately stricter than STS's max-loss margin requirement.
